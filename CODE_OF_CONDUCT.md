# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep
discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub (a private security advisory
or the contact details on the maintainers' profiles). Maintainers will review every report, keep the
reporter's identity confidential, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants.
